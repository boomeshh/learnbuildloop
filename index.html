AZ-900: Cloud Concepts (Module 01)

High Availability & Scalability

HA: Minimum downtime using redundant infrastructure.

Scalability: Vertical (Scale Up/Down - bigger VM) vs Horizontal (Scale Out/In - add more VMs).

Reliability & Predictability

Reliability: Automatic failover and disaster recovery.

Predictability: Confidence in workload performance and upfront cost estimation (Pricing Calculator).

Security & Governance

Security: Protect data, identities, and infrastructure at rest and in transit.

Governance: Enforce corporate compliance standards via policies.

Manageability & Sustainability

Manageability: Automation via CLI, PowerShell, ARM, and monitoring.

Sustainability: Right-sizing and deallocating idle instances to cut carbon footprint.
